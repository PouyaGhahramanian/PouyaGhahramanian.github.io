Language models decay as the world moves on. **LLM-OFA** proposes an On-the-Fly
Adaptation (OFA) framework together with **Adaptimizer**, an optimizer for
continual adaptation under temporal drift.

Evaluated across **two decades of news**, and implemented in PyTorch and
HuggingFace.
