**AdaNEN** is a neural ensemble architecture for classifying text streams whose
underlying distribution shifts over time — learning on the fly, in a single pass,
without retraining from scratch.

It improves classification accuracy by up to **8.8%** across **13 benchmark
datasets**. This work grew out of my M.Sc. thesis at Bilkent.
